Olive Clarke Real Estate Company Limited offers Appraisal services for the following uses:

Valuations

Valuation is the process of determining the worth of an asset or property. Valuation is important because it provides prospective buyers with an idea of how much they should pay for an asset or property and for prospective sellers, how much they should sell for.

Insurance

Property Insurance is one reliable and effective means of protecting your property against various risks. An assessment of the property is done and the estimated value is used as a basis to determine insurance coverage,insurance payment, damage level and insurance benefit.
