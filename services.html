Sample Real Estate Company Limited offers Appraisal services for the following uses:

Valuations

Valuation is the process of determining the worth of an asset or property. Valuation is important because it provides prospective buyers with an idea of how much they should pay for an asset or property and for prospective sellers, how much they should sell for.

Insurance

Property Insurance is one reliable and effective means of protecting your property against various risks. An assessment of the property is done and the estimated value is used as a basis to determine insurance coverage,insurance payment, damage level and insurance benefit. At Sample Real Estate Company Limited, we pride ourselves knowing that we exercise fairness and due diligence in our trade thereby ensuring that our clients can conduct their business void of any conflict of interest , barring any unforeseen circumstances.

Land Aquisition

Land acquisition is the process of legally obtaining land for development, investment, or public use, either through purchase, lease, or government expropriation. A real estate valuator's expertise is essential because they assess the land’s true market value, ensuring fair transactions, preventing overpayment, and helping financial institutions set appropriate loan terms.

Mortgage Referrals

Mortgage referrals involve recommending a trusted lender or mortgage advisor to someone seeking home financing. A real valuation expert plays a crucial role by accurately assessing a property's market value, ensuring fair transactions, and helping lenders set appropriate loan terms. Proper valuation protects both the borrower and lender from financial risk, ensuring the mortgage aligns with the true worth of the property.

Real Estate Appraisals/ Valuations

A real estate appraisal is an unbiased assessment of a property's value, conducted by a licensed appraiser. It helps buyers, sellers, lenders, and investors determine a property's fair market price based on factors like location, condition, and comparable sales. Appraisals are crucial for mortgage approvals, tax assessments, and investment decisions, ensuring transactions are based on accurate valuations.
